# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Warhammer 40,000 players using the tool at a physical table, on desktop now and phone later.

## Product Purpose

Pick two Force Dispositions, derive the paired missions, choose layout A, B, or C, and view the official terrain map offline with minimal delay.

## Brand Personality

Tactical, utilitarian, official.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS dashboards, neon or glass gaming launchers, and decorative fantasy UI.

## Design Principles

- Map first.
- Use official terminology.
- Keep the workflow low-friction and on one screen.
- Stay legible in mixed venue lighting.
- Make controls touch-ready.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast, use keyboard-accessible native controls, provide visible focus, keep touch targets at least 44px, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
